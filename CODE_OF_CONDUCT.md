# Code of Conduct

Hot Potato should be a useful, welcoming project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person’s private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.
