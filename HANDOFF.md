# Contributor handoff

Hot Potato's current implementation and setup are documented in the [README](README.md) and [architecture](docs/architecture.md). The [release readiness guide](docs/release-readiness.md) describes local verification and the external-provider, client, and deployment checks still required before a production release.

Keep personal development notes, environment details, credentials, customer records, and unpublished verification artifacts outside the repository. Record reproducible changes and their exact verification scope in pull requests.
